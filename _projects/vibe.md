## **About**

ViBe is a digital space dedicated to transforming passive observation into genuine cognitive engagement. The platform brings together a structured learning environment for learners, automated insights for educators, and an instructional design explicitly built to ensure learners trust the process and feel confident in what they are learning.

## **The Challenge**

While digital learning has solved the problem of access, completion rates for self-paced video courses consistently hover around 10 to 15 percent globally. Most platforms simply deliver content and assume learning automatically follows. Without a human mentor's gaze, learners often slip into passive viewing without active processing, leading to unverified learning outcomes and surface-level compliance. The core challenge is designing a framework that balances behavioral accountability with intrinsic motivation, ensuring that monitoring builds trust rather than anxiety.

---

## **The Platform**

Inspired by the ancient riddles of Betaal to King Vikramaditya—where every faltered answer demands a backward glance—ViBe acts as a digital scaffold anchored in an integrated, three-theory learning science framework.

{% include video-thumb.html id="8ytNdYlK-BU" title="ViBe — Interactive Demo" %}

**Micro-Learning**
Long lectures are automatically segmented at conceptual breakpoints. By consolidating each concept before the next arrives, ViBe manages working memory, reduces extraneous cognitive load, and prevents learner overload, directly applying Sweller's Cognitive Load Theory.

![A ViBe lecture slide]({{ '/assets/images/vibe/vibe-lecture-slide.png' | relative_url }})
*A lecture, broken into focused segments.*

**Smart Assessments**
Active testing events and quizzes are inserted directly into the viewing flow rather than reserved for massed end-of-module review. Testing memory actively during learning strengthens retention significantly more than passive re-reading or delayed assessments, a principle grounded in Roediger and Karpicke's Retrieval Practice Theory.

![Course content sidebar with quiz segments]({{ '/assets/images/vibe/vibe-course-content.png' | relative_url }})
*Every segment, its quiz, and a quick "how are you feeling" check-in, alongside the video.*

**Active Verification**
AI-driven proctoring monitors focus signals, such as tab switches and absence from the camera, in real-time. By carefully balancing this behavioral accountability with the learner's psychological need for autonomy, ViBe is designed to foster deep cognitive engagement and trust, rather than mere surface compliance, addressing the dynamics outlined in Deci and Ryan's Self-Determination Theory. Every automated flag can be appealed and is reviewed by a person, not decided by a machine alone.

**ViBeCrowd**
Within this collaborative ecosystem, learners do not just consume content but actively curate, refine, and validate questions. A student-written question passes an AI screen, then a peer-validation gate, then instructor approval before it is promoted into the graded bank.

**AI-Built Exams**
Full exams can be generated directly from a course's own material, checked for quality, and set to easy, medium, hard, or a mixed difficulty. Standalone exams run on a timer with the same fair proctoring as the checkpoint quizzes, and results export to PDF.

![An AI-generated exam being taken in ViBe]({{ '/assets/images/vibe/vibe-ai-exam.png' | relative_url }})
*An AI-built exam, running with a timer and question palette.*

**Learning Analytics**
Every learner gets a personal view of their own progress, pace, and quiz performance, course by course — the same signal that lets teachers spot confusion early, before it shows up in a grade.

![A learner's ViBe analytics dashboard]({{ '/assets/images/vibe/vibe-analytics.png' | relative_url }})
